# Client logos still needed

These ten client logos on the site are still the low-resolution artwork from the
old site (180 x 180 pixels each). Every other client logo has been replaced with
a sharp version. The PNGs in this folder show what each one looks like today, so
they can be checked one by one and matched against whatever the client sends.

## What to ask the client for

- **Best:** SVG, EPS or AI — vector, so it stays sharp at any size.
- **Otherwise:** PNG at 1000 pixels wide or more.
- **Ask for the colour version**, not the "white", "reverse", "mono" or
  "inverted" one. The logos sit on white cards, so a white logo arrives as an
  empty card. (Abu Dhabi National Paper Mill supplied a white-only file, which
  is why that one card has a dark background.)

## Once the files arrive

Drop them in this folder under the client's name. They get trimmed, placed on
the same 600 x 450 white plate as the rest, saved as WebP into
`public/logos/clients/<slug>.webp`, and the cards stay identical in size.
